Section 5 - Reading Comprehension

Reading A

Estoy comparando dos apartamentos para el próximo semestre.

El primero es más barato y está cerca de la universidad.

El segundo es más grande, pero está lejos de la estación.

Para mí, el primero es el más práctico.

  1. Why is the first apartment attractive?
  2. What is true about the second apartment?
  3. Which apartment is considered most practical?
  4. What is the context of the choice?

Reading B

— ¿Este barrio es tranquilo?

— Sí, es más tranquilo que el centro.

— ¿Y el precio?

— Es menos caro que otros barrios cercanos.

  1. Is this neighborhood quiet?
  2. Compared to what is it quieter?
  3. How is the price described?
  4. What type of language appears repeatedly?

Section 6 - Writing Task

Guided Writing Requirements

  • Compare two homes or neighborhoods.
  • Use más...que, menos...que, and tan...como.
  • Use at least one superlative expression.
  • Use at least two demonstratives (este/ese/aquel).
  • Conclude with a recommendation and reason.

Free Writing

Write 10-12 connected sentences. Use at least six target forms from this week and include one retrieval element from a previous week.

Section 7 - Speaking Prompts

  1. Describe your current neighborhood.
  2. Compare it with another neighborhood.
  3. Use one equality comparison and one superlative.
  4. Point to two options using demonstratives.
  5. Recommend one option for a student.
  6. Defend your recommendation with two reasons.
