Usage Notes

1) Prioritize high-frequency forms

Use these words repeatedly in speaking and writing before adding new low-frequency items.

2) Connect vocabulary to grammar

Pair new words with this week’s target structures to improve retention and transfer.

3) Retrieval over rereading

Cover the English column and produce Spanish forms from memory, then verify accuracy.

Mini Practice Prompts

  1. Say five words from Category 1 and use each in a short sentence.
  2. Create three questions using Category 2 structures.
  3. Write six lines combining vocabulary from all categories.
  4. Do a 60-second partner exchange using at least eight target words.
