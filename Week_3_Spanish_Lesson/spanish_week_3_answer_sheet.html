Section 2 - Sentence Transformations (Answers)

  1. Mi hermano vive en Houston. (Question) → ¿Mi hermano vive en Houston?
  2. Tengo una hermana. (Negative) → No tengo una hermana.
  3. Tú escribes mensajes. (Change to: ella) → Ella escribe mensajes.
  4. Nosotros vivimos en Texas. (Change to: yo) → Yo vivo en Texas.
  5. Su padre es doctor. (Change possessive to: mi) → Mi padre es doctor.
  6. ¿Tienes primos? (Answer yes in full sentence) → Sí, tengo primos.

Step-by-Step Tip

For transformations: identify subject first, then tense/form, then punctuation (especially questions and accents).

Section 5 - Reading Comprehension (Answers)

Reading A

  1. How old is Julia? → She is eighteen years old.
  2. Where does her mother live? → Her mother lives in San Antonio.
  3. How many siblings does she have? → She has two siblings.
  4. When does the family eat together? → They eat together on Sundays.

Reading B

  1. Does the speaker have siblings? → Yes, the speaker has three siblings.
  2. How many brothers are mentioned? → Two brothers.
  3. Does the sister live here? → No, she does not.
  4. Where does the sister live? → She lives in Houston.

Pronunciation Note

Read the dialogue aloud while marking stress and pauses. Oral decoding supports reading comprehension at beginner level.

Section 8 - Cumulative Review (Answers)

Mixed Fill-ins

  1. Yo soy de Perú.
  2. Tú estudias español en casa.
  3. Ella tiene una hermana.
  4. Nosotros vivimos en familia.
  5. Mi madre es profesora.
  6. ¿Tú practicas con tu hermano?

Error Correction

  1. Yo tienes dos hermanos. → Yo tengo dos hermanos.
  2. Mi madre vivo en Dallas. → Mi madre vive en Dallas.
  3. Tu hermana tienen veinte años. → Tu hermana tiene veinte años.
  4. Nosotros come en casa. → Nosotros comemos en casa.
  5. Quien es tu hermano? → ¿Quién es tu hermano?

Common Mistake Warnings

  • Check subject-form agreement before adding details.
  • Use complete Spanish punctuation for questions.
  • Recycle previous-week structures accurately during cumulative tasks.
