Section 5 - Reading Comprehension

Reading A

En mi ciudad hay un parque grande y una biblioteca nueva.

La biblioteca está al lado de la escuela central.

El hospital está detrás del mercado municipal.

Los taxis están delante de la estación de bus.

  1. What is next to the school?
  2. Where is the hospital?
  3. What is in front of the bus station?
  4. Is there a large park in the city?

Reading B

— Disculpe, ¿dónde está la farmacia?

— Está cerca del banco, a la izquierda.

— ¿Hay una estación cerca?

— Sí, hay una estación detrás del hospital.

  1. Where is the pharmacy?
  2. Is there a station nearby?
  3. Where is the station?
  4. What question word appears in the dialogue?

Section 6 - Writing Task

Guided Writing Requirements

  • Describe at least four places in your neighborhood.
  • Use hay at least twice.
  • Use está/están at least four times.
  • Use at least three different prepositions.
  • Add one question asking for location.

Free Writing

Write 10-12 connected sentences. Use at least six target forms from this week and include one retrieval element from a previous week.

Section 7 - Speaking Prompts

  1. Ask your partner where three places are located.
  2. Answer with full prepositional phrases.
  3. Use one sentence with hay and one with están.
  4. Give one short direction sequence.
  5. Confirm understanding with a follow-up question.
  6. Close the interaction politely.
