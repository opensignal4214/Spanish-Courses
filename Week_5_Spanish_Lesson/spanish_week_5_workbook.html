Section 5 - Reading Comprehension

Reading A

Hoy como en un café cerca de mi escuela.

Me gusta la sopa del día y también me gusta el jugo natural.

Mi amiga quiere una ensalada y puede pagar con tarjeta.

Nosotros pedimos agua sin azúcar.

  1. Where does the speaker eat today?
  2. What two drinks/foods does the speaker like?
  3. What does the friend want?
  4. How do they pay?

Reading B

— Buenas tardes, ¿qué desea?

— Quiero una sopa y un té, por favor.

— ¿Le gusta el pan integral?

— Sí, me gusta. También quiero agua.

  1. What does the customer order first?
  2. Does the customer like whole-grain bread?
  3. What additional drink is requested?
  4. Is the exchange polite?

Section 6 - Writing Task

Guided Writing Requirements

  • Write a café order of at least six lines.
  • Use gustar at least twice.
  • Use quiero/puedo at least three times total.
  • Include one price question.
  • Include one sentence with con or sin.

Free Writing

Write 10-12 connected sentences. Use at least six target forms from this week and include one retrieval element from a previous week.

Section 7 - Speaking Prompts

  1. Say what foods and drinks you like.
  2. Ask your partner what they want to order.
  3. Ask one price question.
  4. Use one polite request with puedo.
  5. Respond to one preference with a follow-up.
  6. Close the order interaction naturally.
